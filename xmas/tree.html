Family tree

 

The family forest

One Christmas tree for each mum and dad — them on top, then their kids, grandkids and great-grandkids. Swipe sideways for the next family. 🎄 next to a name jumps to that person's own tree.

Not on the tree yet

Tree builders

🌳 One tree for the whole family — whatever you put in, everyone sees straight away. Tap anyone to set their mum, dad and partner. Kids join up by themselves once their mum or dad is set. Got something wrong? Anyone can fix it. ⭐ 5 gold stars for each person you put on the tree for the first time.

The questions come from the family tree, so the more of the tree is filled in, the trickier it gets. A cousin's kid is your "first cousin once removed". Two kids whose parents are first cousins are "second cousins".
