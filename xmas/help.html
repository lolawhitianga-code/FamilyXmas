At the beach

🏖️ Beach safety

  • Swim between the red and yellow flags.
  • Bateau Bay Beach out the front of camp isn't patrolled. Shelly Beach and Toowoon Bay have surf clubs.
  • Caught in a rip? Stay calm, float, and raise an arm so someone sees you.
  • Bluebottle sting? Rinse with seawater, pick off any tentacles, then soak in hot water (as hot as is comfortable) for 20 minutes.
🚩 Flags and conditions: beachsafe.org.au

Summer

☀️ Hot weather and fire

  • Slip, slop, slap: shirt, sunscreen, hat.
  • Keep water up to the oldies and the littlies.
  • On a total fire ban day, no fires, no campfires, no gas barbecues outside.
